FAQ

Party questions, answered

How long is a Zoomz birthday party show?

Most party shows run 30 to 45 minutes — the sweet spot for keeping kids dancing the whole time.

What ages is the show for?

The show is built for young kids — toddlers through early elementary — and the grown-ups who bring them. Big kids and parents sing along too.

Do we need to provide a sound system?

No. We bring our own PA and microphones. We just need a power outlet and a spot to set up.

Can you play outdoors?

Yes — backyards, pool parties, parks and festivals. If weather forces a change for an outdoor show, rescheduling is free.

How much does kids birthday party entertainment cost?

It depends on show length, duo vs. full band, and travel. Share your budget on the booking form and we'll recommend the best fit.

Do you do church events or VBS too?

Yes. We also lead kids' worship for VBS, church family nights and camps — same energy, bigger message.
