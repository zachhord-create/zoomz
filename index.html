Who We Are

Pure neon.
Zero chill.

The Zoomz are a 2-person, high-voltage 80s-themed act born and raised in Kansas City. We hit the stage in full tracksuit regalia and don't stop until every fanny pack in the room is swinging.

Equal parts nostalgia, spectacle, and sweat, we bring a live show that's equal parts concert, costume party, and cardio workout. You've never seen anything quite like it.

From intimate bar nights to massive festival stages, we turn every crowd into a neon-soaked, shoulder-pad-wearing family. Come for the music. Stay for the chaos.
